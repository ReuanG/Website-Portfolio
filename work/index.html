Team production / Editing & VFX

CHAOS MV

A personal music video created with a full production team operating cameras, supplying equipment and helping set up shots. I edited the film in Premiere Pro and completed the VFX in After Effects, adapting the final piece after a change in artist participation.

Premiere ProAfter EffectsApprox. 6 months
The production
A collaborative shoot supported by a dedicated team across camera operation, equipment and shot setup.
My contribution
Video editing in Premiere Pro and VFX in After Effects, including revisions to reshape the final film.
Sound design / Original Music Video
The original cut initially had virtually no audio. I built the sound design in Premiere Pro, creating a realistic, full office atmosphere to make viewers feel as though the artists were working in that environment.
Adapting the final cut
The initial production ran for around four months. When a featured artist withdrew, the film needed to be reworked, adding approximately two months to the project.
